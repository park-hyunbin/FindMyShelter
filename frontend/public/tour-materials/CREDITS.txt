Opo online tour materials

The PBR loader, gradient sky, river normals and tiered conifer geometry
adapt techniques from Walkable City by John Bailey / The Bailey Perspective.
https://github.com/freak419/walkable-city

The asphalt maps come from Poly Haven's Asphalt Track by Dimitrios Savva.
https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_track

The entrance stone uses Granite Tile 03 by Charlotte Baglioni (CC0 1.0).
https://polyhaven.com/a/granite_tile_03

The other photo textures come from Poly Haven via Walkable City:
https://github.com/freak419/walkable-city/blob/main/CREDITS.md

forest: brown_mud_leaves_01
grass: aerial_grass_rock
planks: weathered_planks
